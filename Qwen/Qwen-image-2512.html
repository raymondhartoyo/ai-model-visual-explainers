The prompt-to-pixels journey

Loading the interactive scene. The full architecture is also available below.

  1. Prompt → Qwen2.5-VL-7B: template and tokenize, then encode once with 28 language layers into 3,584-wide embeddings.
  2. Gaussian noise → image tokens: 16-channel latent at ⅛ resolution; pack 2 × 2 positions into 64 values and project to 3,072.
  3. 60 MMDiT blocks: separate text and image streams, 24 heads of 128 dimensions, joint non-causal attention. Q/K/V are recomputed every step.
  4. 50 reference steps: a flow-matching Euler scheduler repeatedly updates the latent; true CFG optionally adds a negative-prompt pass.
  5. VAE decoder → RGB: project to 64, unpack to 16 channels, reverse latent standardization and decode at 8× spatial scale.
